# Plan 2.2: Collisions and Pathing

## Objective
Stop the player from phasing inside enemies and allow enemies to intelligently navigate through open doors.

## Context
- .gsd/SPEC.md
- src/store/gameStore.ts
- src/utils/ai.ts

## Tasks

<task type="auto">
  <name>Block player colliding with enemies</name>
  <files>src/store/gameStore.ts</files>
  <action>

  </action>
