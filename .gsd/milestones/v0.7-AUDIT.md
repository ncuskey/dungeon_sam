## Concerns
- Level generation drops the player immediately into a new floor without a summary screen.
- Web Audio API implementations inject static hardcoded frequency sweeps; might impede sound designer later who provides `.mp3`/`.wav` implementations.

## Recommendations
1. Abstract `soundManager` execution out of standard `gameStore.ts` mutations toward a centralized action dispatcher.
2. Provide pre-built boss arenas guaranteeing Watcher entities upon deep floor progression.
